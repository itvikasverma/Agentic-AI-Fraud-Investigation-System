# Case 001 - Account Takeover via New Device

## Transaction Pattern
Customer normally spends ?50-?100 at grocery stores in New York. A transaction for ?2,500 occurred at an electronics merchant in London from a newly registered device.

## Detected Indicators
- Amount deviation (> 2500%)
- New device
- Location change (New York to London)
- Merchant category deviation

## Investigation
The investigator checked the customer's travel history and found no upcoming trips. They contacted the customer via phone. The customer denied making the transaction.

## Final Outcome
Transaction BLOCKED. Card reissued. Account password reset forced.
